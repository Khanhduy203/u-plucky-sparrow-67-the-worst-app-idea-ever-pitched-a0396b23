Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23): one sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable.

**The pitch:** FridgeWatch is an app that livestreams your refrigerator light 24/7 and alerts you the second it turns off, for homeowners who need to know their leftovers are never truly alone in the dark.

A founder-grade pitch for a product nobody on earth needs: a 24/7 livestream of a light bulb that only exists when the door is closed, delivered with total confidence to homeowners worried about their leftovers' feelings.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` is Node-stdlib-only and writes the entry sentence to `<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output`), with no trailing newline.
